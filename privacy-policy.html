Privacy Policy - Sunkidz LMS

Last Updated: March 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Sunkidz LMS ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide our educational management services:

  • User Account Information: Email, password, name, date of birth
  • Educational Data: Student information, attendance records, marks, assignments
  • Location Data: GPS location (only during bus tracking, with permission)
  • Photo Data: Profile photos, gallery uploads (stored securely)
  • Device Information: Device ID, OS version (for push notifications)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

  • Providing and improving our educational services
  • Managing student records and attendance
  • Sending notifications and updates
  • Bus tracking and student safety
  • Complying with legal obligations

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure password hashing with bcrypt
  • JWT token-based authentication
  • Database encryption at rest
  • Regular security audits and updates

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT share your personal data with third parties except:

  • OneSignal - for push notifications (if enabled)
  • WhatsApp/UltraMsg - for SMS/WhatsApp notifications (with consent)
  • Legal requirements and law enforcement when required

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of notifications

Contact us at support@sunkidz.org for any data requests.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is designed for use by school staff and parents managing student information. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 except through parent/teacher accounts. If parental consent is required, parents must authorize all data collection.

8. Location Services

Bus tracking features use GPS location data only with explicit user permission. Location is:

  • Collected only during active bus rides
  • Not stored permanently (real-time tracking only)
  • Not shared with unauthorized users

9. Push Notifications

We use OneSignal for push notifications. Users can opt-in/opt-out at any time in app settings. Notification opt-out does not affect core app functionality.

10. Data Retention

We retain data as long as:

  • Your account is active
  • Required by law (typically 7 years for education records)
  • You request deletion (with some exceptions)

11. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with:

  • OneSignal: Push notifications - https://onesignal.com/privacy
  • Google Fonts: Font delivery - https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • UltraMsg: WhatsApp integration - https://ultramsg.com/privacy

Each has their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. You will be notified of significant changes via email or in-app notification. Your continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of changes.

13. Contact Us

For privacy concerns or data requests:

  • Email: support@sunkidz.org
  • Website: https://sunkidz.org
